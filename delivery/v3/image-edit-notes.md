Remove only the outdoor background, sky, trees, buildings, pavement and ground shadow. Keep the entire actual generator and its metal base, including lifting bracket at top, full shape and perspective, metallic texture, doors, vents, hinges, controls, sockets, StarPower logos and visible ECO+, STAGE V, 45 KVA and SILENCED markings. Preserve photographed product details; do not redesign, change dimensions or invent components. Frame the whole product with a small transparent margin. Output a PNG with true alpha transparency, no floor, no backdrop, no white rectangle, no checkerboard drawn into the image.

Validated: RGBA PNG, 1448 × 1086 pixels, alpha range 0–255.
